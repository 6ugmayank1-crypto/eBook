Frequently asked questions

What is this eBook about?

A step-by-step plan for beginners to pick one skill, find paying clients and work toward a first $1,000 online, mainly through freelance services.

Is it suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. It is written for people starting from zero experience and zero audience.

Do I need experience to get started?

No. The book shows how to audit skills you already have and build samples in about a week.

Do I need to invest money?

The book suggests a budget of $0 to $50; most of what it recommends is free. You do need a computer, internet and a way to receive payments.

No. No income is guaranteed. Results depend on your effort, skills and market, and many beginners need 60 to 90 days or longer.
